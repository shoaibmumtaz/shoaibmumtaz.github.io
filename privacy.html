Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Minto Studio respects your privacy. This policy describes the information that may be handled when you use our games, websites, and related services.

1. Developer and contact information

Developer: Minto Studio
Privacy contact: mintofun18@gmail.com

2. Information we may process

Depending on a game's features and the services it uses, information may include details you choose to share, such as a name, email address, feedback, or support request. We may also receive technical information, including device model, operating-system and app version, language settings, IP address, permitted device identifiers, gameplay interactions, crash reports, and performance data.

3. Why we use information

We use information to operate and improve our games, respond to support requests, diagnose errors, protect our services, understand feature use, measure performance, and meet applicable legal obligations. We aim to process only what is reasonably needed for these purposes.

4. Advertising, analytics, and third parties

Some games may use third-party tools for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, authentication, customer support, or similar technical functions. Those providers may handle information under their own privacy policies. Where tracking permission is required by a platform, we will request it before using applicable tracking technologies.

5. Information sharing

We may share information with service providers only when needed to operate our services, such as cloud, analytics, advertising, security, and support providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users, our services, or our legal rights. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

6. Permissions and platform services

Our apps may request device permissions, such as notifications, photos or media, camera, microphone, location, or network access, only when a feature needs them. You can manage many permissions in your device settings. Platform services, including services provided by Apple, are governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Security, retention, and deletion

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described here, unless law requires or permits a longer period. If a game provides accounts, you may request deletion of eligible account information by emailing mintofun18@gmail.com.

8. Children and privacy choices

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent. Contact us to make a privacy request.

9. International processing and policy updates

Information may be processed in countries other than your own. When required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers. We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change; the date above will be updated when we do.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Minto Studio at mintofun18@gmail.com.
