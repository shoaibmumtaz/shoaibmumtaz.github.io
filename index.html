About the Studio

A small team with big ambitions.

Founded in 2019, Minto Studio is a collective of designers, engineers, artists, and storytellers obsessed with the craft of play.

We believe games are more than entertainment — they're shared memories, emotional journeys, and tiny universes built with care. Every pixel, every line of dialogue, every note of music is placed with intention.

From atmospheric indie adventures to ambitious narrative RPGs, we build worlds players want to live in.

Craft First

Every decision serves the experience. We polish until it feels right.

Player-Centered

We design for emotion, wonder, and the quiet moments in between.

Indie Spirit

Independent, self-funded, and proudly weird. We make what we love.

How We Work

From spark to ship.

01

Concept

We prototype fast, fail fast, and chase the feeling. If the core loop isn't fun in a gray box, it won't be fun in final art.

02

Pre-Production

Vertical slice. Art direction locked. Technical risks resolved. We know what we're building before we commit to building it.

03

Production

Bi-weekly playtests, internal reviews, and a culture of honest feedback. The team plays the game — constantly.

04

Polish & Launch

The last 10% is 90% of the work. We ship when it's ready — and we ship it right.

05

Live & Evolve

Community-first post-launch. Patches, content, and listening. The conversation doesn't end at release.
